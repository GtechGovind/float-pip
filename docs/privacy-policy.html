Float PiP privacy policy

Effective date: November 16, 2026. Applies to version 1.0.0.

Publisher: Govind Yadav. Website: https://qurkos.com/.

Float PiP collects and transmits no personal data. It has no analytics, advertising, accounts, tracking, remote scripts or external services.

When you open the extension controls, the extension inspects video readiness, dimensions and visibility in accessible frames of the current tab. This information is processed temporarily in memory to choose a video. When you activate PiP, the extension clears the selected document's video PiP restriction hints and observes player replacements until that document unloads. It may display a temporary page button when the browser requires a direct click. Website content, video URLs, credentials, cookies, and browsing history are not stored or transmitted by the extension.

The extension requests two permissions: `activeTab` for temporary access to the tab after invocation, and `scripting` to run its bundled video helper. It requests no persistent access to all websites. It does not use browser storage or an external server.

There is no collected user dataset to sell, share, export or delete. Reload the video page to remove the injected helper and observer. Remove the extension using your browser's extension manager.

The video website and your browser operate under their own privacy policies. The extension does not alter those policies. Browser stores may collect installation or usage statistics independently of this extension.

For questions, contact the publisher through the support contact listed on the browser store listing.
