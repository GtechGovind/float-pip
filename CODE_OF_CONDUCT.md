# Code of Conduct

Float PiP welcomes contributors of all backgrounds and experience levels. Treat people with respect, offer useful feedback, give credit and make room for different perspectives.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, deliberate disruption and sharing private information without permission are not acceptable. These expectations apply to repository issues, pull requests, discussions and other project spaces.

Report concerns privately to Govind Yadav at **govind.csae@gmail.com**. Include links and context where possible; do not share someone else's private information publicly. Reports will be handled with care and disclosed only as necessary to respond.

The maintainer may edit or remove harmful content, warn participants, close or lock discussions, or restrict project participation. Serious or repeated misconduct may lead to a ban. Maintainer decisions should reflect the conduct and its effect on the community. Concerns about a decision can be raised privately through the same contact.
